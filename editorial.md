# Editorial record

Status: full read completed against the manuscript hashed in editorial-review.json.

## Contribution and reader

For a reader who has met the argument that modernization strips the socially useful part of an institution while leaving its material dependencies standing, and has met it only as an assertion. The seed material for this paper stated that argument carefully, distinguished the three judgements it requires, sketched a formal model in an appendix and said in terms that the model demonstrates logical possibility rather than an empirical result. This paper computes it. Confidence is high for what the model implies, which is arithmetic under stated assumptions, and absent for anything empirical: nothing here is fitted, no company is named, and the one historical case is documentary and was verified independently of the model.

## Material worth preserving

The appropriability move. Naming the property that makes the capacity to remain the first thing cut, and borrowing the name from the economics of innovation rather than inventing one, is what turns a sociological intuition into something computable. It also supplies the paper's cleanest null: set appropriability uniform and the operator's ordering and the social ordering become identical, rank correlation exactly 1.000. Everything the paper claims lives in the variation of that one parameter, and a reader can check it in a line.

The parking result. The model was not built to produce it and it is the strongest vindication of the seed's slogan. Socially, the first thing a site should shed is the parking its own transaction requires; privately it survives to sixth, three places after tables and chairs. They kept the car and lost the place comes out as a derivation.

The lead. The canary metaphor is the title, and a title that asserts a metaphor should have to pay for it. Nowhere to sit at rent 13.2, doors closed at 81.9, and at the moment the last seat goes the site is still earning 0.588 of peak profit. The sentence that matters is that closure is the lagging indicator and nobody collects the leading one.

The court record. This is the find of the paper and it was not in the seed in usable form. The seed asserted that Black customers were confined to kitchen windows, which is true and verbatim; but it missed the larger thing, which is that the district court held the five drive-ins outside Title II *because* they had no seating and sold half their food for consumption elsewhere. The capacity to remain was the statutory trigger. Designing it away was a defence, and it worked at first instance. A model of why sites shed the ability to stay found its own mechanism written into a 1966 opinion about barbecue.

## Changes the review made

The brief claimed the two orderings were close to reversed. They are not; the rank correlation is 0.556. The claim was withdrawn before it reached the manuscript and replaced with the narrower one that survives computation. This is the second time in this workspace that writing a claim into a brief before computing it has produced a false claim, and the discipline that caught it was writing the number down.

The first draft cited eight of thirty entries. For a paper the seed framed as critical theory that was a real defect, not a bookkeeping one: the model was doing all the work and the tradition was decoration. Two sections were added, each giving specific work to specific authors, and the citation count is now thirty of thirty.

A claim bound 1.000 to the ensemble's maximum rank correlation and failed the gate. The gate was right. The 1.000 in that sentence was the uniform-appropriability reference value; the maximum is 0.822, and the sentence now says so, which is more informative than what it replaced.

Twelve voice diagnostics were triaged and all twelve rewritten. Four sentences were shortened to break a run of thirty-one without a short one.

## What a hostile reader should attack

The ten functions. They are an itemisation of what a roadside site provides, and they are the paper's own; a different list gives different magnitudes throughout, and the ensemble does not vary the list. Someone who thinks "meeting others without buying" is not a function a restaurant provides has a real objection, and the honest answer is that dropping it removes the most extreme loss ratio in the model.

The exact ordering claim. It holds in 0.538 of ensemble draws, which is the weakest of the four robustness figures and is reported as such. The robust versions are that the first cut is a way of staying in every one of 800 draws and that all five sit within the first six in 0.971.

The social values. Someone had to choose them, and the choice determines which ordering counts as the public one. The paper supplies the machinery and not the weights, and says so in a normative claim rather than smuggling it.

The historical section's reach. The 1966 holding was reversed within a year and called erroneous by the Supreme Court within two. Nothing here claims the exemption survives. The claim is that the incentive structure the case reveals is general, and a reader who thinks one repudiated district court opinion cannot carry that weight is entitled to the objection.
