# Brief

## Question

A drive-in can be criticized for the land, fuel and asphalt it consumes and mourned for what disappears when it closes. The seed material for this paper resolves that into a hypothesis it calls selective rationalization: competitive redesign can remove low-yield opportunities to remain in a place while leaving intact the automobile dependence through which the service is delivered. It states the hypothesis as a sign pattern, private return up, social capacity down, ecological burden unchanged, and says explicitly that its formal model "demonstrates logical possibility, not an empirical result." Nothing is computed. So: under what conditions is that sign pattern the generic outcome of profit-maximizing choice rather than one possibility among many, in what order does a site shed the things it provides, and does the loss of the capacity to remain arrive before or after the closure that everyone notices?

## Claim

Four results, each a property of an explicitly stated model rather than an estimate of any real site.

1. **A site sheds its functions in order of private yield density, and the five ways of staying are exactly the first five it sheds.** Give each function a footprint in space and time, a social value, and an appropriability, the share of that value the operator can charge for; private return is the product of the last two, so appropriability is the only substantive stipulation. The operator drops functions in increasing order of private return per unit footprint, a public evaluation in increasing order of social value per unit footprint. The two orders are not reversed, and the paper does not claim they are: their rank correlation is positive. What they are is differently arranged in a specific and damaging way. Every way of staying is cut before anything transactional, while the thing a public evaluation would cut first is the parking that the transaction itself requires, which the operator keeps until sixth. The seed's slogan, that they kept the car and lost the place, comes out as a derivation rather than an assertion. When appropriability is uniform across functions the two orders are identical, which locates the whole result in that one variable.

2. **The canary is exact rather than decorative.** Because slack is the first margin cut and closure is the last, the loss of the capacity to remain precedes the closure of the business by a computable interval of rent. Closure is a lagging indicator of rent pressure; the disappearance of somewhere to sit is a leading one. This is the paper's title claim, and the model turns it into a number.

3. **Removing the capacity to remain removes almost none of the ecological burden.** The operator optimizes over what the operator pays for, and the footprint of a car-oriented site is dominated by the trips made to reach it, which the operator neither pays for nor controls. The model computes the share of total burden that any redesign can move. Because that share is small, the seed's sign pattern is not a special case: it is what profit-maximizing redesign generically produces. The model also computes how often modernization improves everything at once, because a theory that cannot approve of an improvement explains nothing.

4. **Attaching obligations to the capacity to remain makes removing it more attractive.** This is the paper's least comfortable result and the one with a documented instance. In *Newman v. Piggie Park Enterprises* the district court held that five drive-in restaurants fell outside Title II of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 because they had no seating to speak of, served food in disposable containers, and sold half of it for consumption off the premises. The obligation not to discriminate attached to on-premises consumption; a site with nowhere to stay arguably owed nothing. The Fourth Circuit reversed and the Supreme Court called the reasoning erroneous, but the incentive the case exposes is general and computable: any duty that keys on the capacity to remain lowers the rent at which an operator removes it.

## Kind

Formal-model. A stipulated mechanism model, not an estimate: exhaustive enumeration where the space permits, a seeded ensemble for robustness. `claims_target` is the bound ledger in claims.yaml. The history is documentary and separately verified; the model is not fitted to it and does not estimate it.

## Cornerstone literature

The seed's conceptual spine, kept: rationalization and its digital intensification (Ritzer; Ritzer and Miles), the social production of space (Lefebvre), automobility as a system that a change of retail format leaves standing (Sheller and Urry; Urry; Gorz), social reproduction (Fraser), the method of reading a commercial fragment (Benjamin), and the question of ends against perfected means (Horkheimer). Rent and redevelopment (Marx on building-site rent; Smith's rent gap; Harvey), with Aalbers as the discipline that stops "financialization" from being used for ordinary rent pressure. The correctives that stop this becoming nostalgia: Williams on locating the good world just before the present, Jameson against Dika on whether nostalgic forms confront or sedate, Sorin on the car as protection under segregation, Cobble on service work as work.

Two additions the seed did not make, both load-bearing for the formal argument. Appropriability, from the economics of innovation (Teece), is the exact name for the property that makes the capacity to remain the first thing cut: the operator captures a small share of the value it creates. And organizational slack (Bourgeois; Cyert and March), which is a different concept wearing the same word, and must be distinguished rather than borrowed. Oldenburg on third places and Whyte on observed use in small urban spaces supply the empirical vocabulary for what is being lost.

The documentary anchor is the 1966 district court record in *Newman v. Piggie Park*, its reversal at 377 F.2d 433, and the Supreme Court's per curiam at 390 U.S. 400.

## Discipline

No number in the manuscript that is not bound to a JSON pointer in a recorded execution, except numbers taken from documents, which are attributed to the document. No claim that any real drive-in closed for the reason the model gives. The paper does not assert that capitalism cannot tolerate what it cannot monetize; the seed already retracted that and the model shows why it is false, since sufficiently appropriable sociability survives any rent. Where the seed's paraphrase of a source is wrong, the source wins.
